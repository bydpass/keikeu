Refine this folded-paper icon for keikeu, a private writing app, into a cool-toned conceptual art object. Preserve the asymmetrical open folio silhouette and single diagonal folded plane; remove the dark outlines, red corner, primary blue and Bauhaus graphic treatment. Two cool-white paper planes form a quiet abstract sculptural study, one pale ice-blue recessed triangular plane, softly grey-blue edge thickness. Restrained cold monochrome palette, matte porcelain-paper surface, soft controlled directional illumination, subtle contact depth, clean large unbroken planes, no glossy glass, no grain, no glow. Sculptural negative space matters more than decoration. On a full square flat pale blue-grey field, centered optically, mark occupies 72 percent of width with generous margins. Readable as folded writing paper at small size, not a building. No text, no outer rounded tile, no mockup. A thoughtful minimal conceptual paper sculpture with a distinctive silhouette.
